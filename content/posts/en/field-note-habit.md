There is not much time on site. So a note that is **short and accurate** lasts longer than one written to be perfect. Leaving three lines beats writing nothing while chasing the right sentence. A habit survives at the smallest unit you can still do on a tired day, not as a grand system.

## The three-line template

1. **Where** — the place or situation
2. **With whom** — who you met, their role
3. **What remains** — the impression, the question, what to do next

Those three lines are enough to bring the day back a few days later. They are raw material you later shape into writing. If needed, a fourth line marking "fact / interpretation" with one word each helps. With that mark, it is easier to filter out exaggeration and speculation when you publish.

## Why write it right there on site

Go home and tell yourself you will "organize it later" and you will mostly not organize it. You are tired, other things get in the way, and the memory has already been edited. The car on the way home, a five-minute break, the moment right after the meeting — those short gaps are the most accurate.

Writing immediately does not require beautiful sentences. Wrong spelling is fine. Abbreviations are fine. What matters is **putting down an anchor before time runs**. With an anchor you can add flesh to it in the evening; without one, the evening becomes invention. An invented memory drifts away from the evidence on site.

## Keep the tool simple

Pick one thing: a pocket notebook or your phone's notes. Using more than one tool turns "where did I write that?" into a new problem. I prefer to write fast on site and move it into one organizing note before the day ends. If you use AI, let it help with structure at this step only, and I check the factual relations myself.

Repeating the same template makes your hand faster. A template is not a frame that kills creativity; it is a safety net for tired days. When the form is remembered by the body, the content can focus on the site.

## A record is not a performance

A field note is not a performance review. You do not have to exaggerate, and you may write that you do not know. An honest record helps the next plan. A sentence that helps you a few days from now is better than one that makes you look good to someone.
